Thanks for testing Bloblings

You are playing an early version of a tiny colony sim. Hatch bloblings, keep them fed and happy, and watch a colony grow. Play however you like for a while, then tell me how it went — the questions below take about five minutes.

If you have never opened it

The game teaches itself, so you can skip this. The short version:

1

Tap the egg. It only hatches when you tap it, a few times.

2

Feed them. Pick Feed, tap the ground, and hungry bloblings walk over to eat.

3

Watch the colour. Bloblings turn red when they are unhappy. Pick Pet and tap a red one.

4

Clean up. Droppings upset everyone nearby. Pick Clean and tap them.

5

Fill the colony, then retire it from the menu to bank Bloom, and spend it on upgrades that carry into your next colony.

Where it is heading

The colony sim underneath is finished. What comes next depends a lot on what you all say above, but the shape of it looks like this.

More reason to start again. Every colony should feel different from the last, through the traits your bloblings inherit and the surprises that turn up while they live.

A world that moves. Weather and a day-night cycle, so a colony at dusk does not look like a colony at noon.

Something to show for it. Hats, colours and family trees, so a long-lived colony carries its history visibly.

Nothing that nags. No timers, no energy, no punishment for being away, and no ads that interrupt you. Your colony simply naps while the app is closed.

Genuinely, thank you for playing something unfinished. It helps more than you would think.
