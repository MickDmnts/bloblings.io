A colony that grows while you watch it

It starts with one egg. Tap it until it hatches, and a blobling wanders out into the meadow. It gets hungry. It gets lonely. It leaves a mess. Look after it and it grows up, and a well fed, happy blobling eventually lays an egg of its own.

Grown bloblings take on jobs — farming, cleaning, cheering the others up — so a colony you have tended well starts running itself while you watch. They age. Elders slow down, and eventually pass on peacefully, which makes room for the next generation.

No two colonies go the same way. Some grow fat and lazy on a well run farm, some scrape by on your attention alone, and some quietly fall apart while you are not looking. Whichever it is, it happens at its own pace, and none of it is in a hurry.

Four things you can do

Pet

Cheer up a blobling. They turn red when they are unhappy, so you can spot who needs you.

Feed

Drop a snack on the ground. Hungry bloblings walk over and help themselves.

Clean

Sweep up droppings before they upset everyone standing nearby.

Inspect

Look at one blobling: its needs, its age, its job, and how close it is to an egg.

Two ways to play

Seasons

the default

Your colony has room for a few bloblings. Fill it, then retire it to bank its Bloom and spend that on lasting upgrades — roomier colonies, hardier bloblings, workers who know a trade. Every colony after starts stronger.

Zen

no scoring

No cap worth chasing and nothing to earn. Just a colony that keeps going for as long as you feel like looking after it, growing into its jobs on its own.

What it will never do

  • No timers or energy. Nothing asks you to come back on a schedule.

  • No punishment for being away. Your colony simply naps while the app is closed.

  • No ads that interrupt you. There is one optional video, tucked at the bottom of the shop, and you never have to touch it.

  • Nothing attacks your colony. The only way to lose one is to stop looking after it.

The closed beta

Bloblings is playable and being tested by a small group on Android. The colony sim underneath is finished; what happens next depends a lot on what testers say.

  1. Ask for an invite. Testing runs through Google Play, so it needs the email address on your Play account.

  2. Install and play for a while. An hour across a few sittings is plenty. Play however you like.

  3. Tell me how it went. The feedback page has a short questionnaire that copies your answers to the clipboard, ready to paste back.

Blunt is more useful than polite. The things that annoyed you or confused you are the most valuable part.

Where it is heading

  • Bloblings that pass traits to their children. Breed a bloodline: hardier eaters, tidier blobs, sunnier moods.

  • Voices. Each blobling chirping in a pitch of its own, so a colony sounds as alive as it looks.

  • Weather, and day turning to night. Dawn, dusk, rain, and blobs that slow down in the dark.

  • Surprises while a colony lives. A wanderer asking to join, a berry bloom, a cold snap.

  • Something to show for it. Hats, colours, and family trees going back to your very first blobling.

What gets built first depends on what the beta says, so if you are testing, your answers genuinely decide the order.
